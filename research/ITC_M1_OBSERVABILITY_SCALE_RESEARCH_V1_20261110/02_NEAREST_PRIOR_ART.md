## Novelty conclusion

No distinct M1 architectural or mathematical mechanism is currently established. The useful new project decision is to separate lossless encoding from degraded observation before invoking observability. That experimental distinction is not itself claimed as a new vision method.

The next claim to test is a concrete strong-carrier failure under information-equivalent encodings. Even a positive result would still need (i) a causal isolation within the carrier, (ii) a faithful closest mechanism-level control, (iii) net actual-instance gains including losses/FP, and (iv) suitable validation before original-method entry.
